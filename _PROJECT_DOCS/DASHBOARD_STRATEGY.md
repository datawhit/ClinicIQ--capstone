# Dashboard Strategy

This document defines the experience strategy for the main dashboards in ClinIQ. Each dashboard should serve a distinct user need while preserving a shared language of clarity, action, and trust.

## 1. Student Dashboard

### Primary widgets
- Daily priority queue
- Patients needing follow-up soon
- Recent activity affecting the current caseload
- AI-recommended next actions
- Milestone or graduation readiness summary

### KPIs
- Open urgent items
- Cases needing follow-up today
- Pending documentation or workflow steps
- Progress toward graduation or milestone objectives

### AI insights
- What deserves attention first
- Why an item is urgent
- What should happen next

### Notifications
- Urgent case reminders
- Pending task nudges
- Workflow changes that affect the student’s caseload

### Quick actions
- Open highest-priority patient
- Review pending documentation
- Start a workflow task
- Open AI guidance

### Recommended layout
- Top: daily summary and next best action
- Middle: priority work and outstanding items
- Bottom: recent activity and related context

### Daily workflow
1. Review the top recommendations
2. Open the highest-priority case
3. Complete or update the next action
4. Return to the dashboard to confirm the day’s focus

### What should be visible immediately
- What needs attention first
- Why it matters
- What the student should do next

## 2. Faculty Dashboard

### Primary widgets
- Students needing review or intervention
- Cases with elevated risk or stalled progress
- Recent updates from the student caseload
- AI-generated coaching or oversight signals
- Faculty action queue

### KPIs
- Students with unresolved concerns
- Students with stalled progress
- Cases needing faculty attention
- Trends in student activity or risk

### AI insights
- Who may need intervention
- Why the system is flagging a concern
- What the faculty member should examine next

### Notifications
- Student risk escalation
- New follow-up requests
- Workflow exceptions that require review

### Quick actions
- Open a student overview
- Review a flagged case
- Leave or assign guidance
- Escalate a concern

### Recommended layout
- Top: overview of student risk and attention needs
- Middle: active review queue and coaching signals
- Bottom: recent changes and trends

### Daily workflow
1. Review the student attention queue
2. Open the highest-priority student or case
3. Confirm intervention or coaching need
4. Close the loop with a recommendation or next action

### What should be visible immediately
- Which students need attention
- Why they need attention
- What the faculty member should review first

---

## 3. Operations Dashboard

### Primary widgets
- Active bottlenecks
- Workflow exceptions
- Team workload pressure
- Operational health summary
- AI-recommended intervention areas

### KPIs
- Open bottlenecks
- Average resolution time for exceptions
- Workload pressure by team or workflow
- Repeated friction points

### AI insights
- What is likely causing the issue
- Which problems deserve immediate attention
- Where intervention will have the greatest impact

### Notifications
- New operational exceptions
- Escalated blockages
- Decreasing or worsening workflow health

### Quick actions
- Open affected workflow or case
- Escalate an issue
- Review trend context
- Assign ownership

### Recommended layout
- Top: overall operational health and urgent issues
- Middle: workflow exceptions and bottlenecks
- Bottom: trend context and intervention opportunities

### Daily workflow
1. Review the current health summary
2. Identify the highest-priority operational issue
3. Open the affected workflow or case
4. Coordinate a response and monitor progress

### What should be visible immediately
- Where the workflow is breaking down
- What needs intervention first
- Why it matters now

### KPIs
- Cases at risk of delay or disruption

- Items requiring urgent follow-up
- Trends in financial or administrative friction

### AI insights

- Why a case needs attention
- What action would reduce risk

### Notifications
- New high-risk items
- Cases with delayed workflow stages
- Escalations requiring review

### Quick actions

- Open a risk summary
- Escalate for follow-up
- Mark a case as reviewed

### Recommended layout

- Middle: high-priority review items

## 5. Executive Leadership Dashboard

### Primary widgets
- Institutional health summary
- Emerging risks and opportunities
- Trend overview
- High-level operational indicators
- AI-generated leadership summary

### KPIs
- Current health of key operations
- Change over time in workflow performance
- Emerging high-impact concerns
- Areas of improvement or risk

### AI insights
- What is changing and why
- Which trends deserve attention
- What is most important right now

### Notifications
- Major issues requiring leadership attention
- Significant shifts in performance or risk
- Escalations from operations or faculty views

### Quick actions
- Drill into a concern
- Review the underlying operational context
- Open a detailed view for follow-up

### Recommended layout
- Top: strategic health overview
- Middle: major risks and opportunities
- Bottom: detail and drill-down access

### Daily workflow
1. Review the health summary
2. Identify the most important issue or trend
3. Open the underlying context
4. Decide whether action or follow-up is required

### What should be visible immediately
- The current state of the institution’s clinical operations
- The most important risks or opportunities
- The next decision or area of focus
