# Feature Roadmap

This roadmap organizes ClinIQ 2.0 into a phased product strategy aimed at increasing user value and business relevance over time.

## Phase 1: Student Command Center

### Objectives
- Establish the daily experience for students
- Make priority work visible and actionable
- Reduce cognitive load at the start of the clinic day

### User value
- Helps students understand what needs attention first
- Makes case review faster and more focused
- Provides a clearer connection between current work and next actions

### Business value
- Creates a highly visible daily-use product experience
- Builds trust in the product as a core workflow tool
- Strengthens retention and product relevance

### Dependencies
- Core user model and role framework
- Case and patient data visibility
- Basic AI guidance layer

### Estimated complexity
Medium

### Success metrics
- Daily use by core student users
- Improved clarity in task prioritization
- Higher confidence in the next best action

---

## Phase 2: Faculty Intelligence

### Objectives
- Support faculty oversight and intervention workflows
- Surface student progress and risk signals more clearly
- Help faculty focus on meaningful coaching actions

### User value
- Helps faculty spend less time gathering context
- Makes student risk visible earlier
- Improves coaching and intervention quality

### Business value
- Increases product value for faculty stakeholders
- Strengthens adoption across academic programs
- Improves the platform’s credibility as an operational decision tool

### Dependencies
- Student command center foundation
- Role-based workflow logic
- Better summarization and explanation capabilities

### Estimated complexity
Medium

### Success metrics
- Faculty use of oversight views
- Faster recognition of student risk or incomplete work
- Higher satisfaction with coaching support

---

## Phase 3: Operations Intelligence

### Objectives
- Expand the platform into operational visibility and bottleneck detection
- Help administrators and operations teams act earlier
- Turn operational activity into trusted guidance

### User value
- Reduces avoidable workflow friction
- Improves daily operational coordination
- Helps teams address issues before they grow

### Business value
- Creates strategic value beyond student workflows
- Positions ClinIQ as a broader operations platform
- Strengthens the case for institutional adoption

### Dependencies
- Core workflow data model
- Exceptions and bottleneck logic
- Cross-role reporting structure

### Estimated complexity
High

### Success metrics
- Reduced time to identify operational issues
- Better visibility into workflow exceptions
- Higher confidence in operational decision-making

---

## Phase 4: Administrative Analytics

### Objectives
- Provide leadership and administrators with clearer insight into performance and trends
- Support planning and prioritization with summarized operational intelligence
- Connect daily workflows to institutional outcomes

### User value
- Makes performance easier to understand at a glance
- Gives leadership a better view of what is changing and why
- Helps teams align around what matters most

### Business value
- Increases strategic relevance for administrators and leadership
- Strengthens the platform’s role in decision support
- Expands product value across the institution

### Dependencies
- Operational intelligence foundation
- Reporting and summarization capabilities
- Trustworthy data quality and governance

### Estimated complexity
Medium

### Success metrics
- Leadership adoption of summary views
- Better clarity around workflow trends
- Greater use of analytics in planning and review

---

## Phase 5: Institution Configuration

### Objectives
- Make the platform configurable across institutions and program types
- Define reusable configuration patterns for roles, workflows, and language
- Reduce the cost of expanding beyond a first client

### User value
- Makes the product more adaptable to different institutional needs
- Improves fit and usability across contexts
- Reduces friction in adoption and rollout

### Business value
- Creates platform scalability and commercial viability
- Supports future growth beyond a single use case
- Improves long-term product leverage

### Dependencies
- Stable core platform capabilities
- Strong configuration and governance model
- Well-defined institutional workflows

### Estimated complexity
High

### Success metrics
- Ability to support multiple institution-specific configurations
- Reduced customization effort for new deployments
- Greater consistency across implementations

---

## Phase 6: Marketplace and Integrations

### Objectives
- Expand platform reach through integrations and extensibility
- Support richer workflows across systems and partners
- Build around the core intelligence layer rather than isolated point solutions

### User value
- Connects ClinIQ with tools users already rely on
- Reduces duplication and context switching
- Improves workflow continuity across the institution

### Business value
- Increases platform stickiness and ecosystem value
- Creates new opportunities for partnerships and growth
- Extends value beyond the initial product surface

### Dependencies
- Stable platform architecture
- Interoperability and integration strategy
- Institutional readiness and governance

### Estimated complexity
High

### Success metrics
- Successful integrations with core institutional systems
- Reduced friction across workflow handoffs
- Higher platform engagement over time
